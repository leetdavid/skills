# Skills

Shared terms used by the workflows in this repository.

## Language

**Commander's intent**:
A brief expressing the purpose, key tasks, and desired end state of work. It gives people room to adapt their approach within agreed constraints.

**Purpose**:
The reason the work matters and the larger outcome it supports.

**Key tasks**:
The essential actions whose omission would defeat the intent. They are a subset of the work, rather than a complete execution plan.

**Desired end state**:
The observable conditions that define successful completion, including any conditions that must be preserved.
