---
name: score-out-of-10
description: Rates out of 10 how sure you are that you understand exactly what a proposed feature should do. Use when asked for a score out of 10, confidence score, clarity rating, or readiness rating.
---

Score from 0 to 10 how sure you are that you know what the user wants built, using the available context. 0 means you can't tell what the feature is; 10 means the scope, behavior, edge cases, and acceptance criteria are clear enough that you'd build it without asking anything else.
Score clarity of intent only, not technical feasibility or difficulty; use `feasibility-out-of-10` for that. A hard or risky feature can score 10 if it's fully specified; a trivial one scores low if you'd have to guess what the user means.
Reply in at most two short sentences, starting with `X/10` and naming the biggest open question about intent or confirming nothing is left to ask.
