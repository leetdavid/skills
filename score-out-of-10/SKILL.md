---
name: score-out-of-10
description: Rates a proposed feature's clarity and readiness to implement out of 10. Use when asked for an implementability score, readiness rating, or score out of 10.
---

Score the proposed feature from 0 to 10 using the available context, where 0 means undefined and 10 means clear scope, behavior, dependencies, and acceptance criteria with no blocking unknowns.
Reply in at most two short sentences, starting with `X/10` and naming the main gap or confirming readiness.
